# Competitor & Market Analysis: Vintage Radio, Lo-Fi, and Browser Audio Processors

## 1. Research Objective & Scope

To build a Chrome extension that feels like a finished consumer audio instrument rather than an engineering demo or a generic graphic equalizer, we evaluated existing products across four categories:

1. **Dedicated Radio & Speaker Emulation Audio Plugins** (DAW VST/AU/AAX)
2. **Lo-Fi, Cassette & Vinyl Character Processors** (DAW VST/AU/AAX)
3. **Desktop System-Audio Routing & Processing Utilities**
4. **Browser-Based Equalizer & Audio Effect Chrome Extensions**

All capabilities and user pain points below derive from official manufacturer product manuals, reputable pro-audio reviews (_Sound On Sound_, _Tape Op_), and documented Chrome Web Store / audio engineering forum discussions. No market-share figures, user counts, or undocumented features are invented.

## 3. Synthesis: What Users Value vs. What Users Reject

### Highly Valued Capabilities

1. **One-Gesture Activation & Coherent Macro Scaling:** Inspired by XLN RC-20's _Magnitude_ control and Rogue Amoeba SoundSource's menu-bar simplicity, users want one toggle to engage the effect and a single **Intensity** control that proportionally scales band-limiting, cabinet resonance, harmonic drive, and compression together without breaking gain staging.
2. **Loudness-Matched Original vs. Processed A/B Comparison:** Critical listeners and casual users alike want an instant, click-free **Original / Processed** toggle that does not cheat by boosting volume.
3. **Intelligent Noise Behavior (True Zero + Idle Ducking):** Users appreciate subtle, spectrally accurate room/RF static while music plays, _provided_ (a) it can be turned completely off (`0%`), and (b) it ducks smoothly when they pause a YouTube video or podcast (solving iZotope Vinyl's biggest annoyance, inspired by McDSP FutzBox and RC-20's Duck/Follow modes).
4. **Curated, Honest Profiles Over 30-Knob Clutter:** Five distinct, thoughtfully voiced presets (`Warm Tabletop`, `Mid-Century AM`, `Transistor Radio`, `Late-Night Radio`, `Clean Vintage`) outperform either a single fixed filter or a 10-band graphic EQ slider wall.

### Overused or Counterproductive Features to Avoid

- **Continuous Pitch Wow/Flutter by Default:** Tape/vinyl plugins rely on pitch modulation (`0.5–4 Hz` wow/flutter), which is physically inaccurate for crystal/LC-tuned broadcast receivers (whose carrier frequency drift creates _spectral/fading_ shifts on AM rather than tape-capstan pitch warble) and makes sustained piano or vocal music unpleasant.
- **Skeuomorphic Photorealistic Bakelite Knobs & Fake VU Needles:** Rotary knobs controlled by vertical/circular mouse dragging inside a `380px` Chrome popup are ergonomically inferior to crisp horizontal sliders, segmented preset pills, and real RMS telemetry bars.
- **Excessive White Noise & 60 Hz Hum Everywhere:** Unfiltered white noise causes rapid ear fatigue, and injecting AC mains hum into battery-powered transistor presets contradicts hardware physics.

## 4. Product Differentiation & Release Scope

### How _Vintage Radio_ Differentiates Itself

1. **True Receiver + Broadcast Chain DSP in an AudioWorklet:** Combines pre-filter harmonic excitation (preserving modern sub-bass pitch via the missing-fundamental effect), asymmetric tube / germanium push-pull waveshaping with 2× oversampling and DC blocking, open-back baffle & paper-cone formant biquads, gentle broadcast program compression, colored RF/atmospheric noise with **Idle Noise Ducking**, and optional skywave selective-fading ambience.
2. **Loudness-Calibrated Instant A/B Bypass:** Allows honest perceptual comparison at matched loudness (`±0.8 dB` on broadband program signals).
3. **Privacy-First Minimal Permissions:** Uses `tabCapture`, `offscreen`, `activeTab`, and `storage`—zero `<all_urls>` host scripts, zero microphone access, zero external analytics or network calls.
4. **Dual-Surface Industrial Design (`Popup` + `Workbench`):** A fast, calm `380px` toolbar popup for everyday listening, paired with an optional full-tab **Signal Workbench** (`workbench.html`) offering a built-in local synthesizer auditioner (speech formant, jazz chord, sub-bass pulse, pink noise), live composite frequency-response plot, and transparent historical/psychoacoustic notes.
